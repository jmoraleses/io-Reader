Frequently asked questions

Which formats can I open?

EPUB, PDF, TXT, FB2, RTF, Markdown, HTML and CBZ comics, plus M4B and MP3 audiobooks. You can also save a web article by pasting its link.

How do I add a book?

Tap “Add books” in your library and pick a file from the Files app, or share a file to io Reader from another app. io Reader keeps a copy on your iPhone so you can read offline.

How does read‑aloud work?

Voices are generated on your iPhone. Some natural voices need a one‑time download from the voices screen; after that they work offline. You can delete them at any time to free up space.

How do I sync across my devices?

Turn on iCloud in My space › iCloud, backup and privacy. You must be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on. Your data only travels between your own devices on the same iCloud account.

How do I make a backup?

In My space › iCloud, backup and privacy, tap “Create backup” and save the .ioreader file wherever you like. To recover it, use “Restore a backup”. Audiobooks are not included because of their size.

How do I delete all my data?

In My space › iCloud, backup and privacy, tap “Delete all my data”. If you uninstall the app, iOS also deletes the data stored on your iPhone.
