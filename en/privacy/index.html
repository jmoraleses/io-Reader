Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

io Reader is built so your reading stays yours. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your imported books, progress, reading goal and time, settings, collections, notes and vocabulary are stored only on your iPhone. We have no access to them.

iCloud (optional)

If you turn on sync, io Reader stores a copy of that data in the iCloud Drive space of your Apple Account so you can use it on your other devices. Apple manages that storage under its own privacy policy. We cannot see or read that data.

Voice downloads

Some reading voices are downloaded, when you ask for them, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, that service receives the usual technical connection information, such as your IP address. Nothing about you or your books is sent. The voice then runs on your iPhone, offline.

Web articles

If you save an article by pasting a link, io Reader downloads that page directly from the website you entered. That website receives the usual information of a visit.

Backups and exports

Backups (.ioreader) and notebook exports are files you create and save wherever you choose. io Reader does not send them anywhere.

Deleting your data

Use “Delete all my data” in My space › iCloud, backup and privacy, or uninstall the app. To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › io Reader.

Children

io Reader does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the way io Reader handles data changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Have a problem or a suggestion? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and io Reader version. We reply as soon as we can.
